In just a few years, Georgia has become a destination of choice for European real estate investors. Behind this enthusiasm lie solid fundamentals. Here are the figures I always share with my clients before discussing a project.

## A fast-growing economy

Georgia’s GDP grows by **7 to 10% a year**, one of the fastest rates in the region. This growth is driven by tourism, logistics between Europe and Asia and the arrival of foreign companies.

Public finances remain sound: **public debt stands at around 33% of GDP**, far below most European countries.

## A real estate market still catching up

- **9% average annual appreciation** nationwide;
- **15% a year on average in Batumi**, driven by seaside tourism;
- **10 to 15% annual net rental yield** on short-term rentals.

## One of the lightest tax regimes in Europe

- **0% capital gains tax** after two years of ownership;
- **5% tax on rental income**;
- **no property tax or council tax**.

## Three ways to finance your purchase

Pay cash to secure the best discount, spread payments with the developer’s interest-free payment plan, or use the leverage of a Georgian bank loan at delivery: each option deeply changes the capital required and the final return.
